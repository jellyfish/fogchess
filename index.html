Fog of War Rules

  • Full Piece Sight: You only see squares occupied by your pieces and squares your pieces can currently reach or attack.
  • Pawn Sight: Pawns always illuminate their two forward-diagonal squares (even when empty) plus 1 square straight ahead (and 2 squares ahead from their starting rank if unblocked).
  • Line of Sight: Rooks, Bishops, and Queens illuminate rays up to and including the first blocking piece, and cannot see behind blockers.
  • Capture the King: There is no check or checkmate. Moving into an attacked square is legal. Win by capturing the enemy King!
